About Glam Central

A premium salon in the heart of Siwan.

Glam Central is presented as a luxury women's and men's salon offering hair, makeup, beauty, skin and academy-related services.

✓ Hair: Haircut, extensions, shampoo & conditioning, keratin, perming, smoothing and hair spa.
✓ Beauty: Skin care, waxing, cleanups, manicure, pedicure, nail art and nail filling.
✓ Makeup: Party, engagement, base and eye makeup, plus bridal services.
✓ Men's grooming: Beard trim, colour, styling, shaving, luxury shave and beard spa.
